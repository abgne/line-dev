## Global Market Overview

### LINE Primary Markets
1. **Japan** — Largest market, fierce competition, high quality standards
2. **Taiwan** — Second-largest market, localized content preferred
3. **Thailand** — Fast-growing, humorous style popular

## Japan Market Playbook

### User Preferences
- **Healing**: High work pressure creates demand for emotional comfort
- **Seasonality**: Spring cherry blossoms, summer festivals, autumn leaves, winter snow
- **Keigo culture**: Need both formal and informal versions
- **Quality first**: Prefer fewer but polished designs

### Popular Types
1. Animal characters (rabbits, cats, bears most popular)
2. Healing rounded characters
3. Workplace expression stickers
4. Seasonal limited series

### Success Strategies
- **Description text**: Must use natural Japanese
- **Keigo version**: Provide formal "desu/masu" versions
- **Holiday prep**: Upload 1-2 months in advance
- **Quality priority**: Japanese users have zero tolerance for rough design

### Copywriting Examples
```
Bad:  Cute rabbit stickers
Good: Every day usable! Soft cute rabbit stamps (毎日使える！ゆるかわうさぎスタンプ)
```

### Themes (着せかえ) in Japan

Data points from Japanese theme creators, dated. Numbers are theirs, not LINE's.

**Three business models that show up on the Japanese rankings**
1. **Adult colour / pattern catalogue** (kg, RedLake — [experts.md](experts.md) 12–13): no character, 100+ themes, sells material and mood. Region-neutral because there is nothing to translate
2. **Seasonal cadence + colourways** (まるん — [experts.md](experts.md) 11): a dated theme every month plus the same design in several colours
3. **Character IP extension**: the global top-creators showcase is mostly this (cats, dogs), but note the showcase is one global list — the `/ja` and `/en` pages carry the same 24 themes, so it is not a Japan ranking

**Pricing pattern: double-list**
- ¥120 / ¥250 cannot be chosen when Japan is in the sales regions, so Japanese creators list the same theme twice: a ¥370 Japan version and a ¥120 overseas version (Riiiiiii, note.com; Anie's "230 themes, ~400 listings" is the same practice). A ¥120 theme therefore tells you it is an overseas listing, not that the creator is foreign

**What sells — and what does not**
- ぐらむ (note.com, 50 themes made in Illustrator from a reusable parts library): 17 sold in 27 months, ¥4,576 total. Instagram promotion produced nothing; the only exposure that moved sales was appearing in the new-releases list right after launch. Colour-only variants of one design were rejected in review (1.7). Volume is not a strategy on its own
- The cadence model works for the same reason ぐらむ's IG did not: the store's own new-release slot is the channel, so a monthly release is a marketing calendar

**iOS 26 is a maintenance cost**
- Every theme on sale needed 18 additional 80x56 icons in 2026; Anie (230 themes) reported uploading them one by one because the bulk ZIP upload was not available in the dashboard. Price the catalogue models above with that in mind: 100 themes is 1,800 icons per platform change. Spec and timeline in [theme-specs.md](theme-specs.md) → iOS 26 Menu Icons

## Taiwan Market Playbook

### User Preferences
- **Local memes**: Trending phrases, internet culture
- **Tired-of-life mood**: Resonance with social pressure
- **Holiday-oriented**: Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn Festival especially important
- **Price-sensitive**: Free trial + paid upgrade

### Popular Types
1. Tired-of-life / sarcastic series
2. Local trending phrases
3. Workplace complaints
4. Taiwan-specific cultural references

### Success Strategies
- **Track trends**: PTT, Dcard hot phrases
- **Holiday head start**: Upload 2 weeks before competitors
- **Social media management**: FB/IG traffic funneling is essential
- **Continuous updates**: Key to maintaining momentum

## Thailand / Southeast Asia Market Playbook

### User Preferences
- **Humorous exaggeration**: The more dramatic the expressions, the better
- **Cute animals**: Especially cats and dogs
- **Request style**: High demand for pleading and apology expressions
- **Social sharing**: Facebook influence is enormous

### Popular Types
1. Request / apology series
2. Exaggerated expression packs
3. Cute animals
4. Workplace practical phrases

### Success Strategies
- **Enter competitions**: LINE official competitions are a key exposure channel
- **Facebook management**: Thailand has very high FB penetration
- **Serialization**: One concept extends to multiple characters
- **Exaggerated expressions**: Don't be afraid to go over the top

### Competition Strategy
1. Follow LINE Thailand official announcements
2. Study past winners' styles
3. Prepare a new character specifically for the competition
4. Leverage competition exposure to funnel traffic to other works

## Indonesia Market Playbook

### User Preferences
- **Muslim holidays**: Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha
- **Family themes**: Strong family values
- **Religious elements**: Moderate incorporation of Islamic elements
- **Local language**: Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia) preferred

### Important Notes
- Avoid pig imagery
- Muslim holiday stickers have strong demand
- Hijab-wearing characters are well-received
- Respect local cultural customs
